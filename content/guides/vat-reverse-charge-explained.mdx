# VAT Reverse Charge Mechanism: B2B Accounting Rules Explained

The **Reverse Charge Mechanism** is a specialized Value Added Tax (VAT) accounting procedure applied to cross-border business-to-business (B2B) transactions and specific domestic industries (such as the UK Construction Industry Scheme - CIS).

Understanding how the reverse charge works is critical for finance teams, accountants, and businesses operating in the UK, European Union, and global VAT jurisdictions.

---

## 1. What is the Reverse Charge Mechanism?

Under standard VAT rules, the supplier sells a product or service, adds VAT to the invoice, collects the VAT from the customer, and remits it to the tax authority (HMRC in the UK or state tax authorities in Europe).

Under the **Reverse Charge Mechanism**, the responsibility to account for and report VAT shifts from the seller to the buyer:
- The seller issues an invoice with **0% VAT** (stating *"Subject to VAT Reverse Charge"*).
- The buyer declares both the output VAT (VAT due on the purchase) and the input VAT (VAT recoverable) on their own VAT return.

---

## 2. Reverse Charge Accounting Comparison Table

| Feature | Standard VAT Transaction | Reverse Charge VAT Transaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seller Invoice VAT** | Charged (e.g. 20% added) | **0% VAT** (Reverse Charge noted) |
| **Cash Paid to Seller** | Gross Total (Net + VAT) | **Net Amount Only** |
| **VAT Paid by Buyer** | Paid to seller at purchase | Self-assessed on VAT return |
| **Primary Use Cases** | B2C retail sales & domestic B2B | Cross-border B2B & UK CIS Construction |

---

## 3. How to Calculate Reverse Charge VAT

Suppose a UK business purchases £1,000 of cross-border software services from an EU supplier under the reverse charge mechanism at a 20% VAT rate:

1. **Invoice Received**: £1,000.00 (No VAT charged by supplier).
2. **Output VAT Accounted**: £1,000.00 × 20% = **£200.00 VAT due** (Box 1 on UK VAT Return).
3. **Input VAT Recovered**: £1,000.00 × 20% = **£200.00 VAT reclaimed** (Box 4 on UK VAT Return).
4. **Net Cash Tax Impact**: **£0.00** (for fully taxable businesses).

---

## 4. Key Compliance Rules for Invoicing

When issuing reverse charge invoices:
- Must display buyer's valid VAT registration number with country prefix (e.g. GB123456789).
- Must include explicit legal reference text: *"Reverse Charge: Customer to account for VAT to HMRC under section 55A VATA 1994"*.
